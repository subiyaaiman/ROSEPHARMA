Privacy & Data Handling

At Rose Pharmaceuticals and Healthcare Pvt. Ltd., we respect and value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and disclose personal information when you interact with our website or use our nutraceutical manufacturing services (protein powders, syrups, capsules, sachets, and custom formulations).

By using our website and services you consent to the practices described in this policy. Below is a concise overview of the most important points.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email, phone, company name, and other details you provide when contacting us.
  • Non-Personal Information: Browser, device type, IP address, and analytics (collected automatically).
  • Cookies & Tracking: Cookies are used to improve experience and analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser (may limit functionality).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To Provide Services: Manage inquiries, orders, and our manufacturing services.

  • Compliance: We may use data to comply with legal obligations where required.

3. Sharing Your Information

We share information only as needed: with trusted service providers, for legal compliance, or during business transactions (merger/acquisition). We do not sell personal data.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures (encryption, access controls) to protect personal data. No internet transmission is perfectly secure — please be mindful when sharing sensitive data.

5. Your Rights

  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt-out of marketing communications at any time.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party sites with their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If discovered, we will promptly remove such data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Updated versions will display a revised effective date at the top of the page.
