About Rose Pharmaceuticals

Rose Pharmaceuticals & Healthcare Pvt. Ltd., established in 2008, is a trusted name in nutraceutical third-party manufacturing. We specialize in protein powders, syrups, capsules, tablets, sachets, and custom formulations crafted with safety, quality, and innovation. Our GMP, ISO, and FSSAI-certified facility ensures excellence from R&D to packaging. With 15+ years of expertise, we provide end-to-end solutions, flexible MOQs, and timely delivery.
